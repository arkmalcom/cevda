# Homepage and shared shell

Scope: `/` plus the shell every route shares (nav, footer, headings, buttons, page intro, transitions). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: Dominican parents on phones comparing schools for children 45 días to 10 años. Job: trust the school, then start admission or make contact. Proof: the school's own photos, crest, mission, vision, values, levels, activities, requirements. No invented claims. Constraints: copy, routes, form fields fixed; bilingual; reduced motion honored.

Memorable moment: the draggable polaroid stack of real school photos, ringed by gold-star stickers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Dominican school notebook come alive. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed photo slideshow, program cards, and a centered crest.

OWN-WORLD: Crest royal blue (#2B2F8F) fills whole regions; sunflower amber is the one action color; paper white ground with fine grain; palm green and coral only on stickers. Pill controls, 24px-radius cards and photos, die-cut stickers with a white border, highlighter swipes, and a handwritten margin voice.

STORY: A parent sees real children first, learns what each level gives and who the school is, then finds requirements and one clear path to Admisión or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: a display headline of two lines at most with an amber highlighter swipe on one word, a welcome line, then Admisión (filled amber pill) and Contacto (outline pill). Right five columns: a tilted polaroid stack of the five carousel photos, draggable, with three stickers orbiting it. The floating pill nav sits above. On mobile, the headline sits above the stack.

FORM: Living schoolbook, candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (taken as the pick card over assigned candidate 7), seed key a0867add. Signature interaction: the draggable, cursor-tilted photo stack. Motion grammar: ease-out arrivals, springs only for grabbable objects, one scroll-lit statement, one marquee.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
